Design a landing page for "Pose-Booth AI" — an AI-powered photobooth web app with real-time pose detection.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
Design System: Dark Glassmorphism Prism
Background: Deep dark #0A0A0F near-black
Cards: Frosted glass effect — semi-transparent with white at 5% opacity, blur backdrop, subtle white top border highlight
Accent colors: Violet #A855F7 (primary), Cyan #06B6D4 (secondary), Pink #F0ABFC (highlight)
Text: White #F8FAFC primary, Slate #94A3B8 secondary
Typography: Space Grotesk for all text — modern tech sans-serif
Headings: Large, bold, with prism gradient (violet-to-cyan) on key words
Borders: Very subtle — rgba white 8% opacity, or prism gradient borders on featured elements
Shadows: Deep dark shadows, no harsh drop shadows — use glow for hierarchy
Icons: Minimal line icons, white or prism-colored
Buttons: Primary = solid violet #A855F7 with glow; Secondary = glass card style
Overall feel: Premium AI studio, sophisticated, dark but NOT flat — has depth via glass layers
NO light backgrounds, NO warm colors, NO coral/orange — purely dark + prism cool tones

**Page Structure:**
1. **Navigation bar** — Logo "Pose-Booth AI" left, nav links (Booth, Pose Studio, Frames, About) center, "Try Now" CTA button right — glass background, blurs as you scroll
2. **Hero Section** — Full viewport height, centered layout
   - Overline badge: "EXE101 · FPT University 2026" in glass pill
   - Main headline (huge): "AI Pose Detection" on line 1, "Made Beautiful" on line 2 — key words in prism gradient
   - Subtitle: "Real-time AI pose guidance with glassmorphism photobooth. Strike the perfect pose, every time."
   - Two CTA buttons: Primary violet "Open Photobooth", Secondary glass "Explore Poses"
   - Background: abstract dark background with faint violet/cyan particle dots and subtle grid lines
3. **How It Works Section** — 3 steps in a horizontal row
   - Step 1: "Point Camera" — camera icon, glass card
   - Step 2: "AI Analyzes Pose" — neural network icon, glass card  
   - Step 3: "Get Perfect Shot" — photo icon, glass card
   - Connecting animated arrows between steps
4. **Features Grid** — 4 glass cards in 2x2 grid
   - "Real-time Detection" — YOLOv8 AI, violet glow
   - "Pose Library" — 7+ poses, cyan glow
   - "Smart Scoring" — 0-100 score, pink glow
   - "Multiple Frames" — film strip styles, emerald glow
5. **Tech Stack Row** — Horizontal row of tech badges: YOLOv8, MediaPipe, FastAPI, Next.js, GSAP, Python
6. **Footer** — Dark, "Pose-Booth AI © 2026 · EXE101 FPT University" center
